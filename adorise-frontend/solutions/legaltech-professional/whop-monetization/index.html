Frequently Asked Questions

Can corporate clients pay ongoing legal retainers using corporate credit cards or ACH?

Yes. Whop supports corporate cards, ACH transfers, and automatic tax-compliant invoicing for legal retainers.

How are proprietary legal contract templates protected from unauthorized forwarding?

Document portals enforce authenticated session gating and watermarking, preventing unauthorized distribution of precedent libraries.

What happens when a corporate client terminates their monthly advisory retainer?

The Whop webhook immediately downgrades the account, revoking access to premium clause databases and scheduling queues.

Can a law firm offer multi-attorney group licenses for corporate legal departments?

Yes. Whop allows enterprise seat allocations where general counsel can invite in-house attorneys under a single firm billing account.

Does Whop handle digital tax compliance for international legal consulting?

Yes. Whop serves as merchant of record, collecting and remitting applicable sales taxes and VAT globally.
