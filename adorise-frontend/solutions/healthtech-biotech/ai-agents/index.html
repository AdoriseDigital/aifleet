Frequently Asked Questions

Is this agent architecture fully HIPAA compliant?

Yes. All systems deploy with dedicated Business Associate Agreements, end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption, and zero third-party data retention.

Does the agent provide clinical diagnoses?

No. The agent operates strictly as an administrative intake and triage assistant, collecting symptoms and presenting structured data to licensed clinicians.

Which electronic health record (EHR) systems are supported?

We support HL7/FHIR compliant integrations with Epic, AthenaHealth, Cerner, eClinicalWorks, and custom practice management systems.

How does the system handle patient emergencies?

If a patient describes acute emergency symptoms, the agent immediately prompts them to call emergency services and alerts on-call medical staff.

Can patients complete intake via phone or text message?

Yes. Our agents support both conversational voice telephony and mobile SMS web chat workflows.
