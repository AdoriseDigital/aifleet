Can algorithmic trading desks authenticate directly via API keys issued by Whop?

Yes. Our webhook gateway automatically issues signed API bearer tokens with granular rate limits linked to the subscriber Whop plan.

How does Whop support institutional wire payments and corporate treasury invoicing?

Whop allows automated corporate invoicing, ACH transfers, wire payments, and commercial card processing with unified ledger reporting.

What happens to active trading bot connections if a fund subscription expires?

The edge entitlement validator immediately revokes token authorization at the edge proxy, safely disconnecting streaming market feeds.

Can we offer multi-seat corporate packages for buy-side investment teams?

Yes. Whop supports team seat allocations where fund managers can invite junior analysts under a single master billing plan.

Is Whop compliant with financial data distribution licensing requirements?

Whop acts as the merchant of record handling sales tax, KYC verification hooks, and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.
