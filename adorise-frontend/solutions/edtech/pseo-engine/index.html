Frequently Asked Questions

How does programmatic SEO index our entire curriculum catalog?

We map courses, modules, skill competencies, and career transitions into a hierarchical taxonomy that automatically generates schema-rich course discovery pages.

Is Course and EducationalOrganization schema injected automatically?

Yes. All generated pages include Google-compliant Schema.org Course, Organization, and FAQ microdata to maximize rich search snippet capture.

Can students enroll or start a free trial directly from these programmatic pages?

Yes. Every page features direct enrollment CTA links and interactive lesson previews that route visitors into your LMS onboarding flow.

How do you prevent duplicate content across similar beginner and advanced courses?

Each page renders distinct learning objectives, prerequisite requirements, project portfolio descriptions, and tailored career FAQs.

What LMS platforms are compatible with this ingestion pipeline?

We ingest curriculum data from Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Teachable, Thinkific, and custom PostgreSQL databases.
