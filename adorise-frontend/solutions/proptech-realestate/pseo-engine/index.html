Frequently Asked Questions

How does this programmatic engine ingest real estate MLS data?

We connect directly to RESO Web API feeds or custom CSV/JSON exports, normalizing property fields automatically into our Next.js static generation pipeline.

Can we generate pages for specific zoning codes and commercial asset types?

Yes. The taxonomy matrix maps industrial, office, retail, and multi-family categories across specific municipal zoning classifications.

How do you prevent duplicate content across similar neighborhood pages?

Each page incorporates distinct municipal economic metrics, localized transit data, verified cap rate averages, and unique FAQ schema.

What happens when a property listing goes under contract or sells?

Our automated daily batch pipeline updates listing availability status and lastmod timestamps in chunked XML sitemaps automatically.
