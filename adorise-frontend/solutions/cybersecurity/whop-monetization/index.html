Frequently Asked Questions

Can enterprise security teams pay via corporate procurement purchase orders?

Yes. Whop supports corporate purchase orders, wire transfers, and corporate cards with automated VAT and sales tax invoicing.

How do you prevent unauthorized sharing of proprietary threat intelligence feeds?

Gated feeds enforce API key rotation, IP-binding, and concurrency limits to prevent credential sharing among unauthorized users.

What occurs when a security researcher cancels their monthly threat feed subscription?

Whop dispatches an instant webhook revocation, removing private GitHub repo access and Discord researcher roles immediately.

Can a cybersecurity firm offer team licenses for multi-member SOC analysis units?

Yes. Whop supports team seat allocations where security managers can invite analysts under a single billing plan.

Does Whop manage global tax compliance for international security research sales?

Yes. Whop operates as merchant of record, collecting and remitting applicable software taxes and VAT internationally.
