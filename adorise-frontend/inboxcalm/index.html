Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, workflow, and automated forwarding.

❓ Do my clients or colleagues know I'm using InboxCalm?

Never. InboxCalm only communicates with you. The analysis and 3 drafted responses are emailed directly back to your personal inbox. You simply pick the response you like, copy it, and reply to your client as you normally would. No watermarks, badges, or third-party footers.

🔒 Is my confidential email content stored or used to train public models?

No. InboxCalm runs on an isolated Cloudflare edge architecture with zero data retention. Your email text is processed in-memory solely to generate the threat score and draft responses, and is never logged into a permanent database or used for AI training.

🎁 Can I test it before paying?

Yes! Every visitor receives 3 free hostile email scans per day without providing a credit card. You can use the live scanner above or forward an email to inboxcalm@adorisedigital.com immediately to experience the de-escalation speed firsthand.
