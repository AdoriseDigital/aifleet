About the Author: Sanjay Shharma

Sanjay Shharma is the Founder & Director of Adorise AI (Trendy DigiStore LLC, 30 N Gould St, Sheridan, Wyoming, USA), an author, business leader, and executive coach with over 30 years of corporate management experience spanning technology, manufacturing, financial services, and consulting.
